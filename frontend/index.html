How BorrowHub Works

Quick, safe, and organized exchanges between students in minutes.

01

Browse or Request

Search available items like calculators, drawing kits, and microcontrollers, or submit a custom writing task.

02

Quick Approval

Pick your dates and reason. The owner receives an instant notification to accept your borrow request.

03

Campus Hand-off

Meet up at the library, student center, or department lobby for a quick, hassle-free exchange.

04

Return & Review

Return the item on time, mark the transaction complete, and leave peer feedback for community trust.
